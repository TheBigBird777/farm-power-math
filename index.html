Why these three?

They're the questions that come up first on a rural property: what wire to bury for a long run to a barn, pump or gate; how much battery and solar keeps a shed, coop or internet dish running; and what size generator carries the well pump and freezer through an outage. Each calculator shows its math and its assumptions, so you can check the answer or take it to an electrician.

Farm Power Math is a free planning tool. Results are estimates, not engineering or electrical advice. Check your local code and have a licensed electrician confirm anything before you install it.
